Do You Have Any Questions?

How do I get started with web hosting?

Simply choose a plan, complete the payment and your VPS is deployed instantly. You will receive login credentials by email within seconds.

What is the difference for each plan?

Each plan differs in vCPU cores, RAM, NVMe storage and bandwidth. Higher plans include more resources and priority support.

What kind of web hosting do I need?

For small websites the Starter plan is perfect. For business apps and growing traffic we recommend Professional or Enterprise.

Why do I need a domain name?

A domain name is your address on the internet. It makes your website easy to find and builds trust with your visitors.

Is my website protected from hackers?

Yes. Every VPS includes free DDoS protection, firewall options and free SSL certificates to keep your data secure.

How do I backup my website?

You can create automatic backups from your control panel, or our team can set up scheduled backups for you on request.
